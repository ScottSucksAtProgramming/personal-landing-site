**scott** has nearly two decades of EMS experience, the last ten as a paramedic, specializing in high-risk critical care transport, medical emergencies and stroke care. **scott** is lead author of [*Whole Blood Versus Blood Components in Prehospital Care*](https://doi.org/10.1080/10903127.2025.2538741), published in *Prehospital Emergency Care* and cited in a federal AHRQ evidence review. **scott** presented it in Las Vegas in 2024, at NAEMT’s World Trauma Symposium, where it was named a top two trauma abstract, and at EMS World Expo.

**scott** teaches PALS at Stony Brook and served as lieutenant, captain and then chief of Wantagh-Levittown VAC, where **scott** still runs the controlled substance program and teaches classes on professionalism and mental resilience for EMS providers.

Off shift, **scott** builds software and spends his time time working with artificial intelligence. Current projects include [Medic Time](/projects/medic-time/), a hands-free app that timestamps events during EMS calls, and [CivicPulse](/projects/civicpulse/), an AI agent that answers residents’ questions about Town of Babylon government from official records. 

**scott** also writes the [PoweredByGhosts](https://poweredbyghosts.substack.com/) newsletter and, during an overnight game inside the New York Public Library in 2011, co-wrote [*100 Ways to Make History*](https://books.google.com/books/about/100_Ways_to_Make_History.html?id=chxDYAAACAAJ), now held in its rare books collection.
